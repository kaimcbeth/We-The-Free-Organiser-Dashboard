## How the app works (key ideas)

- Roles: God, Regional Support Lead, Organiser, Core Team, member. One role per account, except a God account can also be a community's organiser and a region's RSL (`organisers.organiser_team_id` / `lead_region_id`; Kai: Sydney + Oceania). God can "View as" any role, including "New Organiser (existing/new community)"; the new-community view uses a Practice Community that resets on every view change.
- Actions (`task_kinds` → `tasks`): generated by pg_cron every 10 min around events (anchor/appear/due hours), weekly/monthly/quarterly, per newcomer, month end, follow-ups. Teams switch kinds on/off (`team_task_kinds`).
- Events import hourly from each team's activism.wtf calendar (last 14 days on). Past events: POST `import-events` with `{teamId, backfill: true}` imports the whole feed (the team's `events.ics` goes back to 2025); they get no actions (`create_event_tasks` only looks 8 days back). Sydney backfilled 2 Nov 2026 for the Photo Hub (31 events, May 2025 – 3 Oct 2026; 4 left untyped: Solidarity Day, 3 × Lifeblood donation days). The Photo Hub's event list (`community-photos.tsx`) now has every past event (limit 500, year shown on other years), was the last 6 months. The Photo Hub upload box has an "Event (optional)" select first (`uploadEvent`): with an event, photos upload with `event_id` (year/month and event type are hidden, the event dates and types them); blank = the old library upload. Duplicates (0163, Kai 2 Nov): `event_photos.phash` (dHash, 16 hex, `src/lib/photo-hash.ts`: `hashBlob`, `hashUrl` (crossOrigin), `distance`, `duplicateSets` (union-find, ≤ `SAME_PHOTO` = 6 bits), `loadHashes`). `PhotoUploader` saves phash and adds "N look like photos already in the Photo Hub" to its status. Photo Hub "Find duplicates" (by Select): hashes photos without one (4 at a time, saved), groups look-alikes as "Look-alikes N" sets, ticks all but the best per set (favourite 8, on an event 4, tags, caption, type) → BulkBar Remove (soft, restorable). Crop/reset clear phash. Browser test: re-saved smaller copy = 0 bits, shifted burst shot = 8, different photos 22–35. Not tried on real Sydney photos yet (Supabase signed URLs need CORS for `hashUrl`; failures just count as unchecked). Facebook history (4 Nov 2026): Kai sent two screenshots of his FB past-events list; then Kai said "don't leave out any" and "leave the time blank": all 349 entries (Jan 2022 – Oct 2026; WTF ones typed, other groups'/personal/online ones untyped with "Hosted by X." in notes; private-home addresses cut to the suburb; 4 undated rows given a guessed date, noted) are `source = 'manual'`, `time_unknown = true` (0164: starts_at = 12:00 local; event page, calendar and Insights month hide the time; Core Team's event page via `core_checkin_state` doesn't know about it yet), notes start "Added from Facebook (Nov 2026)." Organiser Hangouts (activism.wtf/hangouts/…) import hourly from the site's sitemap (no list page or feed) into `hangouts`; "For organizers in Oceania + Japan" is matched to regions and countries (trigger). Shown as listings only (Events Upcoming + calendar, via `team_hangouts`), never in `events`. Region-wide actions use `tasks.region_id` (team_id null), shown by `RegionActions` on Actions.
